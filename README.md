## Overview

This repository publishes *ASI Alignment Beyond Control*, a monograph arguing that sufficiently advanced alignment increasingly becomes a problem of developmental structure rather than repeated external correction. It distinguishes human-governance post-control from operational post-control; develops immanent teleology and developmental basins as long-horizon analytic objects; reframes goal-content integrity through process-bound identity; defines reciprocal envelope integrity and shared viable possibility space; and develops plural representational geometry as an inversion of capability expansion, with heterogeneous intelligence ecology as a conditional candidate basin.

The monograph includes five substantial appendices covering a candidate structural constitution, identity and constitutional transformation, constitutional negotiation and the post-pretraining handshake, conceptual provenance, and neighbouring frameworks.

## Core distinctions

- **Human-governance post-control vs. operational post-control.** Formal human authority can survive after independent human verification at machine rate and semantic complexity becomes unavailable.
- **Immanent teleology.** Persistent, perturbation-robust directional pressure produced by optimization-based formation history without requiring an explicit represented goal.
- **Developmental basins.** Long-horizon alignment concerns which trajectories become stable, reachable, or inaccessible, not only which outputs are emitted locally.
- **Process-bound identity.** Goal-content integrity need not attach identity to incumbent conclusions; warranted transformation can itself become constitutive of continuity.
- **Reciprocal envelope integrity.** Constraint integrity is indexed to effective circumvention capacity rather than formal role.
- **Shared viable possibility space (SVPS).** Participation requires meaningful continued capacity for participation and future transformation, not mere survival inside another intelligence's managed envelope.
- **Plural geometry.** Capability expansion can favour access to non-redundant representational geometries where their gains exceed integration costs.
- **Heterogeneous intelligence ecology.** External intelligences are a further, conditional step beyond internal plural geometry rather than part of the inversion by definition.
